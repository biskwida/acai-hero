# Acai Hero — Design Context & Brief

## Design Context

### Users
Health-conscious locals and tourists in Sofia, Bulgaria, mostly on phones near bul. Vitosha.
Jobs to be done: scan the menu and prices, understand what makes the bowls special, find the
location and hours, and order via Glovo / Wolt / Bolt Food. Audience skews wellness/fitness,
açaí lovers, and people after a fresh, energizing treat.

### Brand Personality
ACAI HERO SUPERFOODS™ — Brazilian, energetic, wholesome, a little heroic.
Three words: **warm, juicy, hero-energy.**
Emotional goals: appetite + vitality + a sunny Brazilian escape on a Sofia street.

### Aesthetic Direction
**"Tropical Editorial."** Warm cream/bone paper, deep açaí-aubergine ink, Amazon jungle green,
with passionfruit-gold and dragonfruit-coral accents. Expressive warm display type, organic
shapes, subtle grain, magazine-grade asymmetric layout. Light theme — food reads freshest and
most appetizing on warm light surfaces.
Anti-references: dark neon, glassmorphism, generic SaaS card grids, purple→blue gradients, gradient text.

### Design Principles
1. **Appetite first** — the food and its colors lead; warm light surfaces make açaí pop.
2. **Editorial confidence** — big expressive type, intentional asymmetry, generous rhythm; not a card grid.
3. **Brazilian soul** — tropical color + organic leaf/fruit motifs, never kitsch.
4. **Motion with purpose** — orchestrated reveals and a living hero; always `prefers-reduced-motion` safe.
5. **Every item earns its place** — the full real menu, clearly priced, easy to scan and order.

## Tech
Vite + React + framer-motion. Hand-crafted CSS tokens in OKLCH (no Tailwind defaults).
Type: **Caprasimo** (display) + **Hanken Grotesk** (body), via Google Fonts.
Designed CSS/SVG art leads; a few swappable stock photos live in `/public/images` (see its README).

## Structure (single animated scroll)
Nav · Hero · Ingredient marquee + stats · Menu (filterable, full real item list) ·
Bestseller spotlight · Build-your-bowl · Story / Why açaí · Visit (address, hours, order links, map) · Footer.
